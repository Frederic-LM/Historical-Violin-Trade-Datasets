# Hôtel des Ventes du Tarn (Albi)

## About the house
The Hôtel des Ventes du Tarn is the auction house of Albi, in the Tarn department, run by commissaire-priseur Philippe Amigues, with estimation days also held in Castres.
Source: [Interencheres (auctioneer profile)](https://www.interencheres.com/commissaire-priseur/maitre-philippe-amigues-hotel-des-ventes-du-tarn-166), checked 2026-11-03.

## Files
One file per sale, `<sale day>_Tarn.csv`, from the house's own results document for that sale as published on
interencheres.com (the PDF of the sale's results, read 14 Nov 2026).
Every lot of the violin family as the house describes it, in French (the house's estimate is at the end of the text, as printed: '- 250/300'). A lot printed without a price is `unsold`.

## Prices
`hammer` = the house's adjudication as printed (no buyer's premium). The documents do not print the buyer's premium.
