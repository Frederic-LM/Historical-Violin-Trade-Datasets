# T2 Auctions (Tarisio's online sales)

T2, the online sales of Tarisio, 2016-2023: the lots held, one file per sale day. Only part of these sales is here.

## About the house
T2 is a division of Tarisio designed for the violin trade: instruments and bows that need restoration, have uncertain attributions or come from a trade workshop, offered at wholesale estimates. Bidding is online, with the same buyer's premium as every other Tarisio sale (20 %; 18 % under its 'First in, Last out, Take 2%' terms).
Source: [Tarisio (own site)](https://tarisio.com/), checked 2026-11-03.

## Columns at this house
As in the Tarisio folder: `title` = the maker's name as the house lists the lot, `type` (extra) = its category,
`price_with_fees` = the house's figure (sale currency mostly not known: the house's US-dollar figure).
