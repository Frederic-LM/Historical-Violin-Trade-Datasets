# Where the auction data come from (data v1.17)

110 882 sold lots of violin-family instruments and bows, from 97 auction houses. Each lot is in one of three groups:

- **primary**: the house's own results page, results document or catalogue;
- **secondary, cross-checked**: the lot comes from another source, and a second source agrees (or the house's own price list gives the price);
- **secondary, placed**: the lot comes from one other source only, which names the auction house and gives the day of the sale, or only its month.

| Group | Lots | Share |
|---|---:|---:|
| primary | 63 082 | 57 % |
| secondary, cross-checked | 18 241 | 16 % |
| secondary, placed | 29 559 | 27 % |

Lots counted in the price analysis: 77 497 (rules in the method).

## Secondary sources

The author's notes; Tarisio's archive; Amati's records; Maestronet; the *Illustrated Yearbook of String Instruments* by Claudio Amighetti; the press: The Violin Times (1893-1907) and The Cremona (1906-1911).
